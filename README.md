# EXP 5: COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS OF DIFFERENT TYPES OF PROMPTING PATTERNS AND EXPLAIN WITH VARIOUS TEST SCENARIOS
# AIM:
To test and compare how different pattern models respond to various prompts (broad or unstructured) versus basic prompts (clearer and more refined) across multiple scenarios. Analyze the quality, accuracy, and depth of the generated responses.

# AI Tools Required:
CHATGPT

# Explanation

Prompt engineering plays a major role in improving the quality of AI-generated outputs. In this experiment, two types of prompts are compared:

---

# Types of Prompting Patterns

## 1. Naïve Prompt

### Definition
A naïve prompt is broad, vague, or unstructured. It gives very little context or instruction to the AI model.

### Example
```text
Write about Artificial Intelligence.
```

### Characteristics
- Short and simple
- Less guidance
- May produce generic outputs

---

## 2. Basic Prompt

### Definition
A basic prompt is clearer, structured, and refined. It provides instructions, context, tone, or formatting requirements.

### Example
```text
Write a detailed article about Artificial Intelligence including:
1. Introduction
2. Applications
3. Benefits
4. Challenges
5. Future scope
Use a formal tone.
```

### Characteristics
- Clear instructions
- Structured format
- More detailed responses
- Better accuracy and coherence

---

# Procedure

1. Define naïve and basic prompts.
2. Select multiple test scenarios.
3. Create both naïve and basic prompts for each scenario.
4. Run prompts using ChatGPT.
5. Record the generated responses.
6. Compare the responses based on:
   - Quality
   - Accuracy
   - Depth
7. Analyze how prompt clarity affects output quality.
8. Summarize findings and observations.

# Test Scenario 1 – Creative Story Generation

---

## Naïve Prompt

```text
Write a sci-fi story.
```

### Output

A robot explored space and discovered a new planet where humans could survive.

---

## Basic Prompt

```text
Write a short sci-fi story set in the year 2200 where humans colonize Mars. Include:
- A robot companion
- A conflict involving oxygen shortage
- Emotional ending
Use descriptive language.
```

### Output

In the year 2200, Commander Elena and her robot companion AX-9 struggled to survive after an oxygen system failure on Mars. As the colony lost hope, AX-9 sacrificed itself to restore oxygen supply, saving humanity’s future on the red planet.

---

# Analysis

| Criteria | Naïve Prompt | Basic Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Quality | Simple | Detailed |
| Creativity | Moderate | High |
| Depth | Low | High |
| Coherence | Basic | Strong |

# Test Scenario 2 – Factual Question

---

## Naïve Prompt

```text
Explain climate change.
```

### Output

Climate change is the change in Earth’s climate due to pollution and global warming.

---

## Basic Prompt

```text
Explain climate change in detail including:
1. Causes
2. Effects
3. Human activities responsible
4. Possible solutions
Use an educational tone suitable for students.
```

### Output

Climate change refers to long-term changes in Earth’s temperature and weather patterns. Major causes include greenhouse gas emissions, deforestation, and industrial pollution.

## Effects
- Rising global temperatures
- Melting glaciers
- Extreme weather events

## Solutions
- Renewable energy adoption
- Reforestation
- Reducing carbon emissions

---

# Analysis

| Criteria | Naïve Prompt | Basic Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Moderate | High |
| Clarity | Low | High |
| Detail | Minimal | Detailed |
| Educational Value | Low | High |

# Test Scenario 3 – Summarization

---

## Naïve Prompt

```text
Summarize Artificial Intelligence.
```

### Output

Artificial Intelligence helps machines perform tasks intelligently.

---

## Basic Prompt

```text
Summarize Artificial Intelligence in 150 words covering:
- Definition
- Applications
- Benefits
- Challenges
Use simple language.
```

### Output

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables machines to simulate human intelligence. AI is widely used in healthcare, education, banking, and automation. It improves productivity, reduces human effort, and enhances decision-making. However, AI also faces challenges such as ethical concerns, privacy issues, and job displacement.

|---|---|---|
| Structure | Weak | Well-structured |
| Completeness | Partial | Complete |
| Readability | Average | Good |
| Informative Value | Low | High |

# Test Scenario 4 – Advice and Recommendations

---

## Naïve Prompt

```text
Give study tips.
```

### Output

Study regularly and avoid distractions.

---

## Basic Prompt

```text
Provide effective study tips for engineering students preparing for exams. Include:
- Time management
- Revision methods
- Focus improvement techniques
Use motivational tone.
```

### Output

## Effective Study Tips for Engineering Students

- Create a realistic study schedule
- Use active recall and short notes
- Practice previous year questions
- Follow the Pomodoro technique
- Take regular breaks for better focus

Consistency and discipline are key to exam success.

---

# Analysis

|---|---|---|
| Relevance | Moderate | High |
| Practicality | Low | High |
| Motivation | Low | Good |
| Depth | Minimal | Detailed |

# Overall Findings

## Observations

- Basic prompts consistently generated:
  - More detailed responses
  - Better structure
  - Improved coherence
  - Higher accuracy

- Naïve prompts:
  - Worked for simple tasks
  - Produced shorter and generic outputs
  - Lacked depth and formatting

- Prompt clarity significantly improved AI performance.
